About the Test

The Reaction Time test measures how quickly you can respond to a visual stimulus. It assesses the speed of the neural pathway from your eyes to your brain and finally to your motor function.

Average reaction times are around 200-250ms. Factors like fatigue and distractions can impact your results. Practice can help improve your focus and speed.
