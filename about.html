Our Mission

Brain Arena is an open-source project dedicated to making cognitive science accessible and engaging. We believe that everyone should have the tools to measure and improve their mental performance through transparent, community-driven, and data-driven challenges.

Open Source & Transparent

Our codebase is public and open for contributions. We believe in the power of community to refine our tests and ensure the highest standards of scientific integrity.

Scientifically Inspired

Our tests are based on classical neuropsychological paradigms used in clinics and laboratories around the world to assess attention, memory, and executive function.
